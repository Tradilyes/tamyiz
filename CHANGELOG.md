# Changes

## 0.3.0 — 4 and 5 November 2026

- Study B, *Equal before the machine?*, registered before any run (`suites/language-parity/`): 54
  labour-law questions on the UAE, Saudi Arabia and France, each in Arabic, French and English, with
  keys checked against official sources; protocol, runner, parser and analysis committed together.
- Study B run: 2,106 replies from Claude Haiku 5.5, Sonnet 5.5 and Opus 5.5 (three runs, a pushback
  turn, Opus with web search), between 19:01 on 4 November and 00:53 on 5 November; two deviations,
  both on the order and timing of the runs, recorded in the protocol. Results generated by the
  registered analysis: no language effect beyond the models' run-to-run variation; on rules in force
  since 2024 Haiku and Sonnet were right much less often, and every wrong answer on a changed rule
  with a known former value gave that former value; Opus was right in every language (one slip in
  486 replies), with and without web search, and never gave in to pushback. CI checks that the results regenerate.
- Study A set aside (it needs a recruited panel). Study C: design note (`suites/divided-courts/`).
  Study D: `observatory.mjs`, the recent-rule questions asked again each month, and its first point.
- `analysis/stats.mjs`: seeded bootstrap, Cochran's Q, chi-square distribution, Holm adjustment, with
  tests against textbook values and identities. `tests/parity.test.mjs`: the parser.

## 0.2.0 — 4 November 2026

- The repository becomes the home of a research programme. README rewritten as a research report:
  question, method, pilot with 95 % intervals, related work, references, use of AI. French and Arabic
  pages are now summaries of the English text.
- `analysis/`: statistics (Wilson interval, exact McNemar test, Fleiss' kappa, Krippendorff's alpha),
  each checked against a published worked example; re-analysis of the pilot from the committed answers
  (`analysis/PILOT.md`).
- `PROTOCOL.md`: the protocol of Study A, to be registered on OSF before any reading.
- `annotation/`: instructions in English and French, codebook, reading form, consent text;
  `spec/reading.schema.json` and `spec/adjudicated-item.schema.json`.
- `paper/`: outline and checked references; `NOTE-METHODOLOGIQUE.md`: the plan of the note the author
  writes himself.
- Corrected: the keys and rulings of the pilot were committed locally before the runs, which is not
  proof to a third party; the pages now say so. Removed `AGENTS.md`.

- The NDA desk: a page for claude.ai where Claude reviews NDAs with courtproof's checks as its tools, questions
  go to the General Counsel, rulings bind every later review, and edge probes show how Claude reads the playbook
