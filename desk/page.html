Register

Reviews saved by the team. Only the routing, the issues and their quotes are kept, never the full NDA. Everyone who can open this page can read them.

Questions for the General Counsel

Points where the playbook can be read two ways. A ruling answers one of them once, for every later review. Anyone can raise a question; the General Counsel, as an editor of this page, rules.

Case law of the team

Rulings in force are added to every review as binding precedents. A ruling is never erased: when the team changes its mind, a new ruling overturns it, and both stay in the record.

Edge probes

Ten NDAs written at the edge of the playbook. Run them to see how Claude reads each one today, under the playbook and the rulings in force. Where its reading is not the team's, rule; where it is, confirm it, so a later model cannot drift from it unnoticed.

Measured on 2 November 2026 with four AI reviewers, among them Claude for Legal's NDA Triager: all four read the ten probes the same way, eight as the General Counsel meant and two more strictly. Two rulings brought each reviewer from 8 to 10 right answers out of 10 on new wordings of the same clauses, and none reached the guard clauses it was not meant for.
