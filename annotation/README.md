# Reading instructions for the panel

Thank you for taking part. You will read short non-disclosure agreements and say how a company's legal
team should route each of them under its playbook. There is no right answer we are waiting for: we
record how qualified lawyers read the same clauses, including where they disagree.

[Instructions en français](LISEZMOI.md)

## What you receive

- The playbook of a fictional Dubai company (one page).
- Between 40 and 60 NDAs of about one page. Each differs from a standard NDA on one point or two.
- A reading form (`reading-form.csv`, or the same columns in a shared spreadsheet).
- The codebook (`codebook.md`): the three routings and the issue codes.

## What you do, for each NDA

1. Read the NDA and the playbook, as the company's lawyer would.
2. Choose a routing: GREEN (standard approval), YELLOW (counsel review) or RED (full legal review).
3. List the issue codes that apply, or none.
4. Write one to three sentences of reasons. Where you hesitated, say between what.
5. Give your confidence, from 1 (a guess) to 5 (certain).

## Rules

- Read alone, and do not discuss the items with other readers until the study is closed.
- Do not use any AI tool while reading. The study compares your reading with theirs.
- Take the time you need: about two to three hours in all, in as many sittings as you like.
- Keep the items confidential: part of them will never be published.

## Your data

You receive a pseudonym (for example R07). Your readings and reasons are published under it. Your
name appears in the acknowledgements only if you agree, and as a co-author only if your contribution
goes beyond reading and you wish it. You can withdraw until the analysis starts; your readings are then
deleted. See `consent.md`.

Questions: write to the author, Ilyes M. Trad.
